# Forget-me-not

## Capitulo 1: Reporte

El año es 2056. El cambio climático hace años que alcanzó el punto de no retorno, mucho antes de que yo supiera pronunciar "Capa de ozono". 
Climate collapse has melted vast portions of polar ice, revealing ancient landmasses and unknown trenches. The Aegir-12, a deep-sea research submarine with a top-tier scientific team, vanished three days ago during a classified expedition beneath the Molniya Trench, an uncharted region under the Arctic Circle.

You are Ikr, a mission specialist aboard the A.N.S. Odin, an advanced search-and-rescue submersible dispatched to investigate. Your background: ex-Navy diver, specialist in subaquatic anomalies, fluent in Russian and sonar code interpretation.

🌊 Scene One: Descending Into Darkness

You are strapped into your harness aboard the Odin, deep blue flickering outside the pressure-glass canopy. The light from the surface fades fast. You’ve passed 2,000 meters and are approaching the coordinates where Aegir-12 last transmitted. The pilot, Lieutenant Rowan, sits silent—his jaw tight, knuckles white on the controls.

“We should be pinging them,” he mutters. “Even if they lost power, we’d get something back…”

Suddenly—your sonar lights up.

An echo. Not mechanical. Not geological.

Organic.

Then… silence.

An automated distress beacon flares to life. You're right above the trench edge.

The descent into Molniya Trench begins.
